# contractor-styles

References (measured in `raw-media/styles/_references/<id>/`): @icnc_films DcEtYFLpPkt, @bellucciproduction
DVwhW_FDcml, @toptierremodelingllc DbOg9xLo_VQ. Copy structure, pace and type, never their words, logos or people.

## Sort the footage

OWNER (talking to camera), CREW (people working, team shots), BUILD (progress: framing, demo, install), FINISHED
(the result rooms), DETAIL (close-ups). Write `work/shots.json` first.

## Looks

**Proof cards (@icnc_films)** - caption preset `box-highlight`. 80 s, 4.7 cuts / 10 s. The owner talks in front of
their equipment or job site; captions are Poppins-style bold white in two sizes (a small line + a big key line), places
and numbers in glowing lime/green caps, everyday key words in sky blue, big claims as caps on a translucent blue box; every number cuts to a clean white card
with the number huge in black ("over $1 million", "in just 30 days") and 2-3 small proof photos; short client or crew
clips as proof; a phone-screen mockup when talking about posts or ads. CTA at the end ("fill out the form below").

**Hype crew (@bellucciproduction)** - caption preset `lime-hype`. 20-30 s, 4.3 cuts / 10 s. Opens on a crew member
climbing or walking at camera with a stacked yellow/white hook ("They / say"); then white Poppins ExtraBold 1-2 words at
a time at chest height; speed ramps and whip pans on every move, low angles, drone overhead,
the whole crew cheering on the roof, logo end card on black. Captions only on the big lines. Punchy SFX on every cut.

**Owner walkthrough (@toptierremodelingllc)** - caption preset `gold-caps` (thin white lowercase lead-ins, big
pale-gold #EDE48A condensed caps for key phrases, a grey blurred third line). 60-90 s, 8.4 cuts / 10 s in the build
section (median shot 0.4 s). The owner introduces and narrates; build progress in very fast bursts (framing, drywall,
tile, cabinets), an X-wipe or blur between phases, before-to-after reveal (same angle), then a smooth tour of the
finished rooms, owner close with "thanks for watching".

Default when the note doesn't say: owner on camera + build footage = owner walkthrough; mostly crew action = hype
crew; ad or results message = proof cards.

## Brand

Use the client's brand kit colors for boxes, highlights and cards (for example a maroon and navy kit stays maroon and navy;
never swap in bright red or royal blue). Their logo on the end card.

## Reply

The look used, cuts per 10 s vs the reference, every number card and claim shown, sounds and sources.
