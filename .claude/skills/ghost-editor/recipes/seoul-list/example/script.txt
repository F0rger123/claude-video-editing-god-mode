Five things about life in Lisbon that will change how you picture Portugal.
In the old town you feel it right away: hills, tiles, every street goes uphill.
After dark the streets are always buzzing: students, workers, tourists, everyone at their own pace.
And the food: pastries, sardines, tiny cups of coffee.
I have never seen people this serious about lunch.
Walking, talking, lingering.
And honestly, it all feels like magic.
